# The player's own car's shop parts (brakes, exhaust tips, rims, spoiler, ...)
# through visits to the Performance Shop in free roam among friends
# (nfsu2-web players' reports: brakes, exhaust tips or spoiler gone after any
# shop, back after a reload; runtime/roam_game.c packs_wake).  A free-roam
# part pack was left whole as soon as one of its models in HIBERNATING had a
# copy of its own in it (often a friend's car's); the player's own model of
# that pack stayed in HIBERNATING and the next shop's take-back lost it.  Now
# only the models with a copy stay there.  From a career save whose car has
# shop parts, among stand-in friends changing stock cars (tests/roam/
# plan_loader.sh), VISITS visits (default 3):
#   NFSU2_DATA=<game> tests/roam/plan.sh <out> 1200 roam_ownparts_shop.txt 47600 8 tests/roam/plan_loader.sh
#     NFSU2_SAVE_SEED=<a career save with shop parts> VISITS=3
# Good: "[roam] ... model(s) back from HIBERNATING" after each visit (with a
# copy in a pack, "N of its M models in HIBERNATING are in it too, left
# there" and the others back), and every dump own_* shows the car's parts as
# own_0 does; no trap, own_end, quit.
# Adapted from th0mk's nfsu2-web tests/scripts/roam_ownparts_shop.txt (MIT;
# its friends came from the browser's roam-crowd tool and its part checks
# from the "mp solids" command, here roam_fakepeer stand-ins and dumps).
0     bot on
+0    waitroam 6000
+0    bot off
+0    log ROAM_INROAM
+1200 dump own_0
+0    loop ${VISITS}
+0    log VISIT the Performance Shop
+0    shopgo CC_PERF_SHOP
+300  tapfe RETURN UI_EngageShopDialog.fng 120 2400
+120  tap LEFT
+30   tap RETURN                      # enter the shop
+0    waitfe GarageMain.fng 3000
+600  tap ESCAPE
+200  tap LEFT
+30   tap RETURN                      # back to Explore Mode: Yes
+0    waitroam 4000
+600  dump own_visit
+0    endloop
+60   status own_end
+10   quit
