# Keys held through a focus loss (runtime/keyboard_mac.m kbdmac_release_all, key_down): the focus loss lets
# every held key go and nothing sticks, but a key still down is pressed again by its next autorepeat.  A key
# pressed with Command held never reaches the game, not even by its repeats, and a key held through a focus
# loss whose repeats carry Command is no shortcut.  (On the Mac a Command press releases nothing: its key's
# repeats are dropped, kb_focus_mouse.)  After th0mk's nfsu2-web tests/scripts/kb_focus_regain.txt (MIT).
